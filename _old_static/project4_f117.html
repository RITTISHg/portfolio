Project Overview

Design Overview

The Night Hawack F-117 is an RC airplane project inspired by the iconic stealth aircraft aesthetic, engineered with a rigorous design methodology. The design process began in MATLAB — running aerodynamic simulations to optimize wing geometry, angle of attack, center of gravity, and thrust requirements before a single physical component was fabricated.

The 3D airframe was modeled in Blender CAD, allowing iterative design refinement with precise geometric control. Mechanical Design principles were applied throughout to achieve military-grade structural integrity capable of withstanding the stresses of RC flight with a 5 kg payload.

The final aircraft achieved a top speed of 30 km/hr while maintaining stable flight characteristics. Autonomous flight control systems were integrated to manage flight stabilization, enabling controlled maneuvers beyond manual RC range.

Engineering Process

Design Phases

PHASE_01
Aerodynamic Simulation
MATLAB scripts modeled lift, drag, thrust requirements, and stability margins. Wing chord, span, and sweep angle were iterated until simulation targets were met.
Tool: MATLAB
PHASE_02
CAD Modeling & Geometry
3D airframe modeled in Blender with full fuselage, wing structures, control surfaces, and payload bay. Simulation parameters translated into precise geometric constraints.
Tool: Blender CAD
PHASE_03
Structural Analysis
Mechanical Design principles applied to ensure structural integrity under flight loads. Spar placement, rib spacing, and material selection optimized for weight vs. strength ratio.
Tool: Engineering Analysis
PHASE_04
Physical Fabrication
Airframe components fabricated from lightweight materials guided by the CAD model. Assembly followed a structured integration sequence from fuselage to wing attachment.
Tool: Workshop
PHASE_05
Control System Integration
Autonomous flight control systems integrated — including stabilization algorithms, servo linkages, and RC receiver calibration for precise actuation of all flight surfaces.
Tool: Embedded Systems
PHASE_06
Performance Validation
Flight tests validated MATLAB predictions. Speed runs, payload tests, and stability assessments confirmed the 30 km/hr and 5 kg targets. Submitted to IIT Shastra competition.
Tool: Field Testing
