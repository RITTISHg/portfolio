System Overview

Project Overview

Shore Energy is a sustainable energy development project focused on harnessing renewable energy through novel mechanical design principles. As Team Lead, this project required managing cross-functional contributors spanning mechanical engineering, electronics integration, and data systems.

The core innovation lies in a novel mechanical method that significantly reduces energy conversion losses compared to conventional systems. Through iterative design and prototyping, the team validated a working system achieving a remarkable 60% reduction in energy costs.

Real-time performance tracking was implemented by integrating sensors and monitoring systems throughout the prototype, enabling continuous optimization and data-driven refinement during testing phases.

🎖 Team Lead Role
Led cross-functional team through full project lifecycle: requirement definition, design iteration, prototype fabrication, sensor integration, and final performance validation.
